Key Findings

Strengths

  • Linear model achieves best RMSE (0.70°C) with simple architecture
  • CNN achieves best correlation (0.81) by preserving spatial locality
  • All models perform well in surface layers (2-30m): avg RMSE 0.44-0.60°C
  • 7-channel satellite input significantly improves over single-channel SST

Challenges

  • Deep water (200-1000m) RMSE reaches 0.77-1.54°C — limited surface-deep coupling signal
  • ViT patch-based approach loses spatial locality → lower correlation (0.34)
  • Bay of Bengal stratification creates higher errors during monsoon season
  • 50-150m thermocline zone is hardest to reconstruct for all models
